EU duty drawback for retailers

Money already paid to EU customs on returned orders may be recoverable.

EU duty drawback lets DTC retailers reclaim import duties paid on goods that are returned by customers, refunded, and re-exported.

Check your eligibility

A quick check

Who qualifies?

Your business may qualify when these conditions are met.

EU-registered importer

You sell direct to consumers from outside the EU.

Orders above EUR 150

The order value is above the refund threshold.

Fair market value

The customs value matches the item's fair value.

Unused and refunded

The customer returns the unused item and receives a refund.

Re-exported in 3 months

The returned goods leave the EU within three months of import.

Simple, managed flow

How the process works

Part A: one-time onboarding

1

Free eligibility review

TDR reviews your import history, documents, and refund potential.

2

Connect your carrier

TDR connects with your carrier, or you choose a pre-integrated carrier.

3

Connect your export broker

Use your broker or one of TDR's pre-integrated brokers.

4

Set up deferment

TDR helps you set up a deferment account with Dutch customs.

Part B: every eligible order

1

Import clearance

TDR clears each import through an approved EU customs procedure.

2

Real-time coordination

We coordinate with your carrier so goods keep moving smoothly.

3

Returns consolidated

Returned items are consolidated in an EU country. In the Netherlands, TDR handles the export declaration.

4

Complete audit trail

TDR matches import and export data for each item.

5

Instant claim

The refund claim is submitted to customs as soon as the file is complete.

6

Money returned

Once approved, duties go straight to your retailer deferment account.

Your part is clear

Your role as a retailer

You stay in control of your customer and sales experience. TDR manages the customs work around it.

  • You are the importer of record for goods entering Europe.
  • You provide a manifest of imported orders under EU customs rules.
  • Manifest values match each item's fair market value.
  • You use an integrated carrier, or share carrier details for validation.
  • TDR handles import clearance; duties use your deferment account.
  • Only unused, refunded, and timely re-exported items qualify.
