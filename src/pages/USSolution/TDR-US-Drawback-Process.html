US duty drawback for retailers

How TDR US duty drawback works for returned orders

US duty drawback helps importers recover duties paid on goods that are later exported, destroyed, or removed from the US market.

Check your eligibility

A quick check

Who Qualifies?

Your business may qualify when these conditions are met.

US importer of record

You import goods into the United States and can provide entry data.

Duties and fees paid

Import duties, taxes, or eligible fees were paid at entry.

Traceable documentation

Entry, invoice, transportation, and export records can be matched.

Unused and refunded

The customer returns the unused item and receives a refund.

Exported within five years

The eligible goods are exported from the United States within the claim period.

Your part is clear

Your role as a retailer

You stay in control of your customer and sales experience. TDR manages the customs work around it.

  • You are the importer of record for goods entering the United States.
  • You provide a manifest of imported orders under US customs rules.
  • Manifest values match each item's retail market value.
  • You use an integrated carrier, or share carrier details for validation.
  • TDR handles import clearance; duties use your deferment account.
  • Only unused, refunded, and timely re-exported items qualify.
  • You use an integrated export broker, or share broker details for validation.
