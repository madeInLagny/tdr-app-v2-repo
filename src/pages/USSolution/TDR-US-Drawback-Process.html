US duty drawback for retailers

How TDR US duty drawback works for returned orders

US duty drawback helps importers recover duties paid on goods that are later exported, destroyed, or removed from the US market.

Check your eligibility

Who qualifies

US drawback can work for your returns flow

US-registered importer

You sell direct to consumers from outside the US as the importer of record.

Imported and re-exported in the same conditions

The consignee did not use the goods and was refunded for the retail value.

Destroyed goods are also eligible

If you destroy stock instead of returning it, you may still be able to reclaim duty.

Claim within five years

Depending on the return reason, you may be able to recover duty from one to five years after import.

From import to refund

The end-to-end flow

Every eligible order is coordinated from the initial shipment through the final customs refund.

  1. 1 TDR

    Daily compliance monitoring

    TDR monitors broker performance and checks that every order remains aligned with the drawback process, catching issues quickly before too many shipments fall off the eligibility list.

  2. 2 TDR

    Collects the required data

    TDR connects with carriers, brokers, and customer ERP systems to pull the information needed to prepare and support the claim.

  3. 3 CBP

    Claims are submitted and approved

    TDR submits the drawback claim on the importer's behalf. Once CBP reviews and approves it, the credited funds are sent directly to the importer’s bank account.

  4. 4 Importer

    Tracks claims in one platform

    TDR has developed a single global platform to monitor drawback claims, giving importers visibility into progress and cash flow impact in real time.
